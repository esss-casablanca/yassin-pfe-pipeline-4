# Rehearsal guide (rehearsal_guide.docx)

A short French document the student follows between the lock and the soutenance. It contains no new
content about the PFE; it organises the practice.

## Four rehearsal sessions
1. **Lecture** — read the script aloud once per slide, without the clock; mark every sentence that does not
   feel natural and rephrase it (the wording of the notes is editable until the lock; after the lock, only the
   oral delivery changes).
2. **Chronométrage** — full run with the timer; compare slide by slide with `timing` in the notes document;
   adjust the delivery (not the content) until the total is within the target.
3. **Questions** — a colleague or family member reads 10 questions from the bank at random; answer each in
   under a minute; note the answers that needed the paper to be re-read.
4. **Répétition générale** — full run in the conditions of the day (standing, clicker or keyboard, the deck
   from the final file), followed by 10 questions, including the three difficult ones.

## Delivery checklist
- Look at the jury, not at the screen; the notes are in the head, the slide is a cue.
- One sentence of transition between sections; silence rather than "euh".
- Every number is said as written on the slide; every limitation is said aloud.
- The answer to a question starts with the direct answer, then the justification, then stops.
- When the answer is not in the work: "Nous ne l'avons pas étudié ; c'est une perspective."

## Day-of checklist
- The final deck (`soutenance_deck_FINAL.pptx`) on two media (USB key + e-mail to self), plus a PDF export.
- Printed notes script and the jury-questions bank.
- A printed copy of each paper with the tables tabbed (annexes answer the jury faster than scrolling).
- Arrive early; test the projector with the deck (fonts: Gill Sans MT and Corbel are standard Windows fonts).
