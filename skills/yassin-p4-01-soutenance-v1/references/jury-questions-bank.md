# Jury-questions bank (jury_questions_bank.docx)

25–40 questions, in French, that an ESSS jury is likely to ask about **this** PFE, with model answers drawn
**only from the two papers** (ledger ids in brackets). Where the papers are silent, the model answer says so and
coaches the honest reply ("Nous n'avons pas mesuré cela ; c'est une limite que nous signalons, et une
perspective").

## Nine categories (cover each; weight by the project)
1. **Problématique et pertinence** — why this question, why Morocco / this setting, what the review added.
2. **Méthodes de la revue** — sources, dates, eligibility, screening (who, how many), appraisal tool, synthesis
   method, PRISMA counts [R-N-…], why no meta-analysis (or why one).
3. **Méthodes de l'étude empirique** — design and why, setting, population, sampling, sample size and its
   basis, instrument (validation, language, reliability [E-N-…]), variables, analysis plan, deviations from
   the protocol and how they were handled.
4. **Résultats** — the primary estimate and its CI, what it means and does not mean, the subgroup and
   exploratory findings, the tables the jury may point at.
5. **Limites et biais** — selection, response, common-method, measurement, confounding, generalisability,
   sample shortfall; what was done to limit each.
6. **Articulation des deux composantes** — how the review question led to the empirical objective, where the
   empirical results agree or disagree with the review, and why.
7. **Implications** — for practice, for the institution, for policy; hedged exactly as the papers hedge them.
8. **Éthique et conduite** — authorisations, consent, anonymisation, data handling, the ethics attestation.
9. **Réflexion personnelle** — what the student learned, what they would do differently, next steps.

## Format of an entry
```
Q07 · Catégorie 3 · Difficulté ★★☆
Question : Comment avez-vous déterminé la taille de l'échantillon ?
Pourquoi le jury la pose : vérifier que l'étude était planifiée et non opportuniste.
Réponse modèle (à dire avec vos mots) : … [E-N-0031, E-N-0032]
À ne pas dire : « parce que c'est ce que nous avons pu obtenir » sans mentionner le calcul prévu et l'écart.
Diapositive d'appui : 14 ; annexe : A2.
```

## Rules
- Every number in a model answer exists in the ledger with the same value; every claim keeps its hedge.
- Each answer is 3–6 sentences the student can say in under a minute.
- Include at least three "difficult" questions (★★★) that probe a real weakness of the work; the honest
  answer to a weakness is part of what the jury grades.
- The bank is appraised and locked with the deck in p4-02 (it is part of the soutenance lock).
